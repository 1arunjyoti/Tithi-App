## TIPS FOR VERIFICATION

1. **Time Zones Matter**: Drik Panchang uses local Indian time (IST). Tithi can change during the day.

2. **Regional Variations**: Some festivals are celebrated on different dates in different regions:
   - North vs South India differences
   - Purnimanta vs Amanta calendar systems
   - Check your specific region

3. **Udaya Tithi vs Entire Day**: Some festivals follow the tithi at sunrise (udaya tithi), which might differ from the date when tithi is present for most of the day.

4. **Multi-day Festivals**: 
   - Navratri: 9 days
   - Onam: 10 days (Thiruvonam is main day)
   - Chhath: 4 days

5. **Fixed Solar Dates**:
   - Makar Sankranti: Usually Jan 14
   - Lohri: Usually Jan 13
   - Baisakhi: Usually April 13-14

6. **Cross-Reference**: When in doubt, check 2-3 sources. If they differ, note which calculation method they use.
